My story

Growing up inside geometry.

I was born in Iran, and I grew up inside geometry before I knew its name.

It was everywhere — in the tilework of mosques and old houses, in the carved plaster of ceilings, in courtyards and gardens laid out in perfect symmetry, in the fabrics we lived with, and above all in the rugs beneath our feet. Long before I could explain it, I knew how it felt: a room built on geometry is calm. Your eye travels, finds the pattern, and rests.

Years later, working across Dubai, London and New York, I understood what I had absorbed as a child. These patterns are not decoration. They are constructed — circle by circle, line by line — from rules that have not changed in a thousand years. That is why they never go out of style, and why they feel as right in a Manhattan lobby as in a Persian courtyard.

Geometry gives me calm, connection and harmony. It connects me to where I come from, and to builders in every culture who found the same forms on their own. It is the most timeless beauty I know.

GeoMera Atelier is how I share it: one geometric language, in colours taken only from nature, carried across every surface of a space — the floor, the walls, the furniture, the fabric. Each design is made for one place and one client, and belongs to them alone.

Ellie Kashani
