Commissions

Geometry needs a director.

A motif on its own is not a design. Where it appears, at what scale, in which colours — and where it deliberately does not — is what makes a space feel calm rather than crowded.

Ellie Kashani directs that from the first floor plan to the final installation, so the geometry keeps its power in every room.

DevelopersResidential buildings where one design language carries every unit and every common space.
HospitalityHotels, residences and restaurants that need an identity guests feel before they read it.
Private clientsHomes with a design that exists nowhere else and belongs to them alone.
Architects & designersStudios who want a signature surface language within their own scheme.

What a GeoMera signature does

A feeling guests can’t name — but never forget.

A busy, characterful interior does not have to be a restless one. Geometry and a palette drawn from nature give even the richest boutique space an underlying order — the quiet harmony that makes a place feel settled, generous and complete.

  1. i. Harmony Most interiors are designed corner by corner, until no two corners belong to the same place. A GeoMera property is designed as one composition: every room speaks the same language, so the whole building feels whole.
  2. ii. Calm Colour taken only from the site’s own stone, soil, planting and light. Guests may not be able to name what they feel — but they feel it, and they settle.
  3. iii. Space One continuous geometry, scaled with intention, lets the eye travel from room to room without interruption. Smaller and boutique properties read as more spacious, more grand, more considered.
  4. iv. Story Each property receives a signature that exists nowhere else, rooted in its place. It feels authentic rather than fashionable — and it grows richer with age instead of looking dated.
  5. v. Recognition The signature is visible in every photograph and every film taken on the property. A guest’s picture becomes unmistakably yours — and memorable long after the stay.

Three ways to work together

From a single motif to a whole building.

I.

Signature Motif

An original geometric motif and palette, created for one project and one client.

  • Motif built from a single geometric construction
  • Palette drawn from the site’s own surroundings
  • Seamless repeat files in agreed scales
  • A usage guide: where, how large, which colours
  • Exclusive use for the named project

For a single room, a textile or a rug.

II.

Geometry Master Plan

Send the floor plans; receive the complete system for the property.

  • Everything in the Signature Motif
  • Room-by-room placement: floors, walls, fireplace, splashback, rugs, upholstery
  • Scale for each space, from lobby to cushion
  • Colour proportions per room, and which colours may meet
  • Transitions, so the whole property reads as one composition
  • Drawings, renders and material specifications

For a home, a residence or a building, delivered to your own team.

III.Flagship

Signature Project

Full direction, from the first drawing to the day the space is handed over.

  • Everything in the Geometry Master Plan
  • Production with specialist makers selected and approved by the studio — tile and stone, rugs, textiles, furniture
  • Sample and colour approval at every stage
  • Production oversight and quality control
  • Site visits during installation, and final sign-off
  • A numbered, signed certificate of authenticity

The client never has to find a factory. Accepted by invitation, a few each year.

The Master Plan

One motif. Five scales. Never too much.

The same geometry appears in every room, but never the same way twice. It is large where you arrive, quiet where you rest, and absent where the eye needs space.

  1. 1Entrance — the motif at its largest, on the floor, in Desert Sage.
  2. 2Living — a rug at middle scale, echoed in a fine border around the fireplace.
  3. 3Kitchen — a single band of splashback; the rest is calm.
  4. 4Bedroom — the finest scale, on the headboard and textiles only.
  5. 5Bath — a full floor at medium scale, returning to the colour of the entrance.

The process

From conversation to certificate.

  1. 01ConversationThe project, the place, the people who will live in it. Plans and site photographs if available.
  2. 02CommissionScope and fee agreed; a deposit reserves the studio’s time.
  3. 03Palette from the siteColours drawn from the stone, soil, planting and light of the place itself.
  4. 04DesignThe motif is constructed, then placed room by room in the Master Plan.
  5. 05SamplesTile, stone, weave and fabric samples approved for colour and craft before production.
  6. 06Production & installationMade by workshops selected for the project, overseen through to installation on site.
  7. 07CertificateA numbered certificate of authenticity, signed by Ellie Kashani, records the design as an original.

What every commission promises

ExclusivityEach design is made for one place. The client holds exclusive use; GeoMera Atelier never repeats it elsewhere.
RestraintGeometry where it matters, silence where it does not. The power of a motif depends on where it is left out.
CraftEvery piece made by workshops chosen and approved by the studio, to the same standard of colour and precision.
ProvenanceA signed, numbered record of authorship, so the design keeps its value for as long as the building stands.

The Signature. GeoMera’s own motif, the Ogee Facet Trellis, is reserved for GeoMera projects and is not available for commission.
