Ellie’s Story

A lifetime inside geometry.

Being Persian, I grew up inside geometry before I knew its name.

It was all around me: in the tilework of mosques and old houses, in carved plaster ceilings, in courtyards and gardens laid out in perfect symmetry, in the fabrics we lived with, and above all in the rugs beneath our feet. Long before I could explain it, I knew how it felt. A room built on geometry is calm. The eye travels, finds the pattern, and rests.

My career in real estate has taken me across Dubai, London and New York. Alongside it, I have travelled wherever ancient builders left their mark, from the stone terraces of Machu Picchu to the pyramids. Everywhere I went, I found myself searching for the same thing. The motifs changed, the materials changed, the centuries changed. The harmony did not.

Standing in those places, I understood what I had absorbed as a child. These patterns were never decoration. They were constructed, circle by circle and line by line, from rules that great builders everywhere seem to have discovered for themselves. That is why they still move us, and why they feel as right in a Manhattan lobby as in a Persian courtyard.

Geometry gives me calm, connection and harmony. It ties me to where I come from, and to every culture that found the same forms on its own. It is the most timeless beauty I know.

GeoMera Atelier is how I share it. One geometric language, in colours taken only from nature, carried across every surface of a space: the floor, the walls, the furniture, the fabric. Each design is made for one place and one client, and it belongs to them alone.

Ellie Kashani
