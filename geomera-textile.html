Pattern and color, drawn from the same principles as everything we build.

Every surface deserves the same intention as the architecture around it.

GeoMera Textile extends our design philosophy beyond architecture and into the objects a space is furnished and lived in with every day — fabric, upholstery, rugs, and the smaller details that complete a room.

Every pattern begins with geometry already present throughout our work. The Flower of Life, the golden ratio, and the faceted proportions of the GeoMera mark are not decorative afterthoughts — they are the same governing systems used to plan a site, and here they are simply given a different scale.

Color is extracted from the land, not from a swatch book. Each palette is drawn from the tones of limestone, clay, sand, and desert light already present in GeoMera's work, then selected by hand and tested against the architecture it is intended to furnish.

Nothing here is generated or applied without consideration. Every print is designed once, in service of a specific project.

GeoMera Textile is available exclusively to GeoMera clients as part of a commissioned project. Every design shown here is proprietary to GeoMera and protected accordingly — not for resale, licensing, or third-party reproduction.

The Sedona Palette

Born from a journey to Sedona, a place truly close to my heart, this palette was inspired by the majestic landscapes of the Red Rocks. After pulling countless shades directly from nature and refining them through endless iteration, I created this nine-color blend: The Sedona Palette.

— Founder, Ellie Kashani
